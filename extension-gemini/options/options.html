CropCal (Gemini)

Click the CropCal toolbar button (or press Alt/Option+Shift+C), drag a box around an event on any page, and a prefilled Google Calendar event opens for you to review and save.

Setup

  1. Get a Gemini API key at aistudio.google.com (free tier, Google account only).
  2. Paste it below, click Save, then Test connection.
